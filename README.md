🌾 KrishiMitra AI
An Offline Multilingual Intelligent Platform for Sustainable Agriculture and Food Waste Management
📌 Overview

KrishiMitra AI is an AI-powered smart agriculture platform designed to assist farmers throughout the entire agricultural lifecycle. The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, and Voice Assistance to improve crop productivity, reduce food waste, promote sustainable farming, and enhance farmer decision-making.

The platform supports multilingual communication and offline accessibility, making it suitable for rural farming communities with limited internet connectivity.
